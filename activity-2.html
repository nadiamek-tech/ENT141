Pitch your team’s Project 2 idea to camera in 30–45 seconds.

1Write your script

Five parts. About 75–110 words.

2Practise with the timer

Stop between 30 and 45 seconds.

3Record with your phone

  • Light: face a window or lamp.
  • Frame: phone at eye level, head and shoulders.
  • Sound: a quiet room, one arm away.
  • One take: no music or editing needed.
  • Check: over 45 seconds? Cut words, not speed.
